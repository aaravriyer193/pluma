Why Pluma

Built to disappear.
Not to distract.

Every design decision trades friction for focus. No new window. No context switch. No mouse.

System-wide overlay

Floats above every app — Figma, Notion, VS Code, your browser. No dock icon. No taskbar entry. Just Alt+P.

Clipboard-aware

The moment you summon Pluma, it reads your clipboard. Whatever you had copied is silently loaded as context.

Auto-paste

Rewrites and transformations paste directly into your focused app. Pluma hides, Ctrl+V fires — you never touch the clipboard.

Whisper Window

Press Tab to expand into a full streaming chat. Context persists across the day. Resets at midnight.

Screen context

Captures a screenshot when summoned. The model sees exactly what you see — ask about anything on your screen.

OS keychain storage

Your API key lives in Windows Credential Manager — never in a config file, never on disk in plaintext.

Slash commands

Type a /command and
watch it happen.

Prefixed commands transform your clipboard and paste the result straight back. No confirmation needed.

/polite

Rewrites clipboard text to be professional and polite.

/concise

Strips fluff. Shortens dramatically while preserving the point.

/tldr

Produces exactly three bullet points. No more, no less.

/bash

Converts plain English into a shell command. Ready to run.

/regex

Turns a description into a regex pattern. Just the pattern.

/explain

Explains code or text simply. No jargon unless you need it.

/translate

Translates clipboard into any language you specify.

/doc

Generates docstrings or JSDoc that match your code's language.
